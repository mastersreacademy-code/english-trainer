# Plan cover artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool for feature 7. Originals remain outside the repository; deployable assets are compressed 800px WebP files. Each plan has a distinct cover; Classes 1 and 5 use school and teen respectively. The three original everyday scenes remain separate panels of learning-worlds.webp.

Common prompt: One landscape editorial illustration for an English-learning plan cover. Premium hand-painted gouache, soft grain, expressive natural people, modern warm educational magazine style. Distinct composition, fill canvas edge to edge. No text, logos, watermark, borders or UI; not a collage.
